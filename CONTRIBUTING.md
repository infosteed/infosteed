# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in improving the project.

External contributions are not accepted yet. The copyright holder and lawyer-approved contributor licence agreement (CLA) have not been finalized. Public pull requests must remain disabled or be closed without merging until the release checklist records both decisions. A Developer Certificate of Origin alone does not grant the relicensing rights required for the intended dual-licensing model.

Once contributions open, every contributor must accept the published CLA before a change can be reviewed. The CLA must grant the named copyright holder sufficient rights to redistribute the contribution under AGPL-3.0-only and under separate commercial terms. The CLA will not transfer ownership of a contributor's copyright unless its final lawyer-approved text explicitly says so.

For now, use issues for reproducible bug reports and design discussion. Do not include private recordings, credentials, access tokens, customer data, or third-party confidential information.

Maintainers should require a clean checkout to pass the checks documented in `docs/release-process.md`. All commits and releases are performed by the repository owner.
